Screening results are decision-support, not findings of wrongdoing.

Softdayta Risk uses public-source records, user-supplied data and AI-assisted summaries to help compliance teams identify items for review. Public preview results may be incomplete and must not be used to deny services, accuse any person, or make legal/commercial decisions without human verification and source review.

Why screening matters

Built for AML, CFT and counterparty risk teams

Screen customers, suppliers, donors, public officials and related parties against a traceable local PIP database, public-source evidence, and adverse-media review queues.

01

Higher-precision matching

Name validation, merged-name splitting, confidence scoring and role evidence help separate real people from page headings, institutions and menu text.

02

Audit-ready evidence

Every candidate can carry a basis, source URL, source excerpt, confidence score, reviewer notes and last-reviewed date.

03

Human review controls

Confirm, dismiss or escalate matches before reliance. The platform supports review workflows rather than automated accusations or decisions.
