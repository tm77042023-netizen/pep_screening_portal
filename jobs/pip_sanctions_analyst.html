PIP & Sanctions Analyst (Close-Family & Adverse Media)

Full-time — RiskSecure Compliance — Hybrid/Remote (Botswana-focussed remit)

Location: Gaborone / Remote (work that engages regional public sources and international reporting). Team: Research & Screening Operations.

About RiskSecure Compliance

RiskSecure Compliance builds pragmatic screening and monitoring tools for financial crime compliance teams. Our PEP/PIP Screening Portal is a lightweight, subscriber-focused Flask application that supports single-name screening, bulk Excel screening, monitoring/watchlists, an admin dashboard for managing PEP/PIP records, ingestion of public-source PDFs and news archives, and an audit-ready review workflow. We serve clients who need reliable, source-backed decisions and curated adverse-media context for onboarding and ongoing monitoring.

Role overview

This role is responsible for maintaining and improving the integrity, coverage and operational workflows around PIP/PIP sanctioning, close-family relationships and adverse-media monitoring. You will be the subject-matter expert who ensures that records, relationships and adverse context in our screening database are accurate, defensible and kept up to date. The position blends investigative research, data stewardship and quality control within the application and ingestion pipelines that power our portal.

Key responsibilities

  • Maintain and curate the PEP/PIP database: verify source material, update `PepRecord` entries, manage aliases, jurisdictions, positions and profile summaries.
  • Manage sanctions and close-family relationships: create and maintain `PipRelationship` records, record evidence and confidence scores, and ensure audit log entries capture reviewer decisions.
  • Adverse media monitoring: review `AdverseMediaSearch` and `AdverseMediaAlert` items, triage alerts, summarise risk themes and recommend reviewer actions.
  • Operate staged import workflows: review `StagedImportCandidate` items produced by PDF ingestion, accept/reject candidates, and migrate approved records into the main dataset.
  • Maintain public source registry: validate `PublicSource` entries, run periodic checks and update source metadata and ingestion schedules.
  • Quality assurance for screening outputs: sample `ScreeningResult` records, tune matching heuristics, and propose improvements to reduce false positives/negatives.
  • Produce and maintain documentation and audit notes: write clear reviewer notes, maintain provenance for decisions and help keep the portal audit-ready for clients.
  • Work with developers and product team to refine ingestion (PDF/web scraping), matching algorithms and exports (Excel templates and reports).
  • Respond to subscriber queries about screening outcomes and provide expert context where needed.
